# RadEye

**RadEye** is a free, radiology-focused educational platform designed to support imaging interpretation and exam preparation for medical students and radiology trainees.

It brings together concise modality-based notes, real-world imaging cases, image-based MCQs, and curated resources in one structured, distraction-free space.

🌐 Live site: https://drsyedsaqibalishah.github.io/RadEye/

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Radiology preparation is often fragmented across scattered PDFs, videos, and notes.  
RadEye aims to simplify this by providing:

- Exam-oriented content
- Clinically relevant interpretation
- Clear structure aligned with how radiologists think
- Emphasis on common pitfalls and mimics

Built **by doctors, for doctors in training**.

---

## 👥 Who RadEye is for

- Medical students
- Radiology residents and trainees
- Doctors preparing for radiology-related exams (FCPS,MCPS,FRCR, MD/MS, PLAB, USMLE)
- Anyone seeking structured radiology learning

---

## 📂 What this repository contains

### 🔹 Content (Notes)
Modality-based learning material:
- X-ray
- Ultrasound
- CT
- MRI

Focused on interpretation, protocols, key signs, and exam pearls.

---

### 🔹 Cases
System-wise imaging cases:
- Chest
- Neuro
- Abdomen
- MSK

Each case is intended to include:
- Brief clinical context
- Key imaging findings
- Differential diagnoses
- Final diagnosis
- Exam and reporting pearls

---

### 🔹 MCQs
- Image-based questions
- Rapid-review exam material
- Focus on pattern recognition and decision-making

---

### 🔹 Resources
Curated references including:
- Recommended books
- Trusted video resources
- Guidelines relevant to radiology exams

---

## 🧭 Project Philosophy

RadEye emphasizes:
- **Clarity over volume**
- **Understanding over memorization**
- **Reporting-style thinking**
- **Awareness of examiner traps**

This is a learning platform, not a dump of files.
